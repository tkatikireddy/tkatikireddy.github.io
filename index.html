01 about

Models are easy to demo.
I make them hold up.

I work on the part of an LLM system that decides whether it survives production: what gets measured before release, what's enforced at runtime, and what you can see when something breaks.

At Optum that means a shared agent platform with inherited guardrails, a trajectory-level evaluation gate, and tracing that turned hours of debugging into minutes. Before that I shipped NLP and ML models into live Salesforce environments and published research at Boston University.

/01

Evals & guardrails

Trajectory-level evaluation gates and runtime policies for prompt injection and PHI/PII leakage.

/02

Agentic retrieval

Complexity-routed RAG, re-ranking and retrieve-reason-verify loops that keep answers grounded.

/03

Fine-tuning & serving

LoRA / QLoRA with PEFT, GPTQ · AWQ · GGUF quantization, and serving on vLLM.

/04

LLMOps & cost

Tracing, monitoring and per-task token ceilings so failures are visible and spend is bounded.
